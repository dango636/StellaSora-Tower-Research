Third-party components and sources

1. Offline UI font subset
   web/assets/stella-ui.woff is derived from Noto Sans CJK.
   Its source, modification details and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are retained
   in web/assets/SOURCE.json and web/assets/LICENSE-Noto.txt.

2. Stella Sora simulation kernel
   The supplied simulation kernel is retained in stella/vendor/stella_sim.py.
   Original provenance and the current rule revision are documented in
   stella/vendor/SOURCE.json and stella/vendor/README.txt.

3. External runtime dependencies
   Python, NumPy, PyTorch, the NVIDIA driver and NVRTC are installed separately.
   Their original distributions retain their respective licenses and notices.
   The optional test suite additionally requires pytest.
